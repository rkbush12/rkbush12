What I do

Consulting that covers the whole data journey

Most analytics projects break at the seams — between the database and the dashboard. I work across all four layers so insight actually reaches the people making decisions.

Data Engineering & ETL

Reliable pipelines that collect, clean, and model your data so every report starts from a single source of truth.

  • ETL / ELT pipelines & scheduling
  • Data cleaning, validation & deduplication
  • Warehouse / lakehouse modeling (star schema)
  • SQL optimization & automation

Power BI & Reporting

Executive-ready dashboards that are fast, good-looking, and built on well-designed DAX measures — not fragile spreadsheets.

  • DAX measures & semantic models

Going past "what happened" to "why" and "what's next" — finding the patterns, segments, and drivers hidden in your data.
